KB 34 — Residential Zone Types

KB item (verbatim from households_kb.jsonl):
  id: 34
  knowledge: "Residential Zone Types"
  description: "Illustrates the types of residential zones based on geographic classification."
  definition: "Includes 'Urban' (densely populated city areas), 'Suburban' (residential outskirts), 'Rural' (sparsely populated countryside), and 'Mixed' (transitional areas). Null indicates unclassified or pending zoning."
  type: value_illustration
  children_knowledge: -1

Reason: The schema has no `residential_zone` column. `locations` carries only `regioncode` (text) and `zonenum` (int); `households.locregion` and `households.loczone` are foreign-key references with no documented categorical mapping to Urban / Suburban / Rural / Mixed. KB 45 states that `loczone = 1` defines an Urban Zone (encoded as `households.is_urban_zone`); no comparable mappings exist for Suburban, Rural, or Mixed.
